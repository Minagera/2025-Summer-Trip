# 2025 Summer Trip: Revive & Drive Rules

## The Concept
"Revive & Drive" is a father-son adventure where we fly one-way to purchase a project vehicle, then fix it up enough to drive it back home across the country. This repository contains our trip planning documents and itineraries.

## Vehicle Requirements

### Must-Haves
- **Budget:** Maximum $4,000 purchase price
- **Age Range:** Between 1970 and 1996 model years
- **Type:** Preferably a truck or SUV, but open to other options
- **Condition:** Must be drivable or fixable within our timeframe
- **Documentation:** Clean/valid title required

### Preferences
- **Transmission:** Manual preferred, but automatics are acceptable
- **Make Priority:**
  1. Chevrolet
  2. Ford
  3. Dodge
  4. Others will be considered if they're interesting or a great deal
- **Fuel Type:** Either gas or diesel is acceptable

## Trip Logistics
- **Flight:** One-way from Huntsville (HSV) using Delta SkyMiles
- **Duration:** Roughly 10-11 days total
- **Tools:** Must be able to fit tools and supplies in the purchased vehicle
- **Route:** Will depend on purchase location, but should include scenic drives and automotive points of interest

## Potential Purchase Locations
We've developed detailed itineraries for:
- Salt Lake City, UT (see [Salt-Lake-Start.md](Salt-Lake-Start.md))
- Seattle, WA (see [Seattle-Start.md](Seattle-Start.md))

## Documentation Plans
- GoPro video recording throughout
- Daily mechanical assessments
- YouTube series documenting the journey
- Before/during/after photography

## The Spirit of the Adventure
The goal is to create lasting memories while putting our mechanical skills to the test. We're looking for a project vehicle with character and potential, not perfection. The journey home is as important as the destination.
