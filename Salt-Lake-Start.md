# Revive & Drive: Scenic Back Roads Edition

**Father-Son Road Trip | Summer 2025 | 10 Days**
Project car purchase and drive home through America's most scenic back roads

---

## Friday June 13: Arrival in Salt Lake City
**Evening**
- Land at SLC airport
- Check into hotel near downtown
- Light dinner and prep for tomorrow

---

## Saturday June 14: Car Acquisition & Salt Flats
**Morning: The Big Purchase**
- Meet seller, thorough inspection and test drive
- Complete paperwork and payment
- Parts run: tools, fluids, emergency supplies
- First maintenance session

**Afternoon: Bonneville Salt Flats**
- Drive to Salt Flats via I-80 (only highway day)
- Sunrise photo shoot with project car
- Test performance on the flats
- Return to SLC for final prep

---

## Sunday June 15: SLC to Capitol Reef via Scenic Byway 12
**Route: UT-89 South through Dixie National Forest**
- **Stop:** Panguitch for lunch (historic Mormon town)
- **Drive:** UT-12 East - America's most scenic highway
- **Stop:** Red Canyon (mini-Bryce with road tunnels)
- **Stop:** Dixie National Forest overlooks
- **Night:** Torrey (gateway to Capitol Reef)

**Distance:** 280 miles | **Time:** 6 hours of stunning scenery

---

## Monday June 16: Capitol Reef to Moab via Cathedral Valley
**Morning:** Capitol Reef National Park scenic drive
**Route:** Cathedral Valley Loop (dirt road through moonscape)
**Route:** UT-24 to UT-95 (scenic desert route)
- **Stop:** Goblin Valley State Park (alien landscape)
- **Route:** UT-128 along Colorado River (incredible canyon drive)
- **Evening:** Moab

**Distance:** 150 miles | **Time:** 8 hours with stops

---

## Tuesday June 17: Arches & Canyonlands
**Morning:** Arches National Park
**Afternoon:** Canyonlands Island in the Sky scenic drive
**Evening:** Dead Horse Point State Park for sunset photography

---

## Wednesday June 18: Moab to Durango via Monument Valley
**Route:** US-191 South to US-163 (Monument Valley Scenic Drive)
- **Stop:** Valley of the Gods (14-mile dirt loop)
- **Stop:** Monument Valley Navajo Tribal Park
- **Stop:** Four Corners Monument
- **Route:** US-160 West through mountains to Durango

**Distance:** 350 miles | **Time:** 8 hours with stops

---

## Thursday June 19: Million Dollar Highway
**Full Day:** US-550 Silverton to Ouray Loop
- **Morning:** Durango to Silverton
- **Afternoon:** Silverton to Ouray via Red Mountain Pass (11,075 ft elevation)
- **Evening:** Return to Durango

**Distance:** 230 miles of mountain scenery

---

## Friday June 20: Durango to Santa Fe via High Road
**Route:** US-160 East to US-84 South
- **Stop:** Chama (historic railroad town)
- **Route:** NM-68 "High Road to Taos" through artist colonies
- **Stop:** Chimayo (famous for chile and weaving)
- **Stop:** Taos Pueblo (1,000-year-old Native American village)
- **Route:** NM-68 South to Santa Fe

**Distance:** 280 miles | **Time:** 7 hours

---

## Saturday June 21: Santa Fe to Colfax Family Visit
**Morning:** Santa Fe Plaza and art galleries
**Route:** Original Route 66 through Albuquerque
**Route:** US-54 South to US-380 East (desert scenery)
- **Optional Stop:** Roswell (UFO museum - open 9am-5pm)
- **Route:** US-285 to US-82 East to US-167 South
- **Evening:** Arrive Colfax area for family visit

**Distance:** 480 miles | **Time:** 8-9 hours

---

## Sunday June 22: Family Time & Drive Home
**Morning:** Visit with family near Colfax, Louisiana
**Afternoon Departure:** 
**Route Home:** US-167 to US-165 to US-84 through Louisiana
- **Stop:** Natchez area (historic river town)
- **Route:** Natchez Trace Parkway (scenic parkway, no commercial traffic)
- **Arrival:** Home in Alabama

**Distance:** 6-8 hours depending on Alabama location

---

## Best Photography Locations
- **Red Canyon:** Road tunnels carved through rock
- **Cathedral Valley:** Towering sandstone spires at sunrise
- **Monument Valley:** Classic western landscape shots
- **Million Dollar Highway:** Mountain passes and cliff-hanging roads
- **Taos Pueblo:** Ancient adobe architecture
- **Louisiana:** Plantation country and family moments

---

## Vehicle Maintenance Checkpoints
- **Day 2:** Initial setup and Salt Flats performance test
- **Day 4:** Post-dirt road inspection after Cathedral Valley
- **Day 7:** High-altitude performance check after Million Dollar Highway
- **Day 9:** Final systems check before family visit

## Emergency Resources Along Route
- **Utah:** Moab Garage Co (restoration-friendly)
- **Colorado:** Durango Motor Company (classic vehicle specialists)
- **New Mexico:** Santa Fe classic car shops
- **Louisiana:** Local family connections for assistance

This route prioritizes scenic beauty over speed, taking back roads through some of America's most spectacular landscapes while testing our project car's performance across diverse terrain.
