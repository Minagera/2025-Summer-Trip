# Revive & Drive Road Trip Itinerary – Starting in Salt Lake City, UT

This father-son road trip combines automotive adventure with scenic exploration. You'll fly one-way from Huntsville using Delta SkyMiles, pick up a pre-purchased project car in Salt Lake City, and embark on the journey of reviving it while driving back home.

**Trip Duration:** ~10 Days  
**Total Driving Distance:** ~1,600 miles  
**Vehicle Note:** Your pre-purchased classic project car (late '70s to early '90s) awaits in Salt Lake City.

## Pre-Trip Planning

### Before You Go
- **Forums & Research:** Join model-specific forums to ask about common issues with your vehicle
- **Community Connections:** Post your route on enthusiast forums to find potential local help
- **Tool Assessment:** Check what specialized tools might be needed for your specific vehicle
- **Documentation Prep:** Set up GoPro mounts, prepare storage for video/photo files
- **Packing List:** Bring basic clothing, but save luggage space for tools and parts you'll acquire

---

## Day 1: Arrival & Vehicle Acquisition

### Morning: Arrive in Salt Lake City
- Land at Salt Lake City International Airport (SLC)
- Grab breakfast while waiting for seller/previous owner
- Take rideshare to the meeting location

### Mid-Day: The Handover
- **Meet with Previous Owner (3+ hours):**
  - Thoroughly inspect the vehicle together
  - Document all known issues (take notes and photos)
  - Test drive with owner to identify quirks and concerns
  - Get specific recommendations for immediate repairs
  - Complete paperwork and payment finalization
  - Create a prioritized list of needed parts/tools

### Afternoon: Supplies Run
- **Harbor Freight Shopping:**
  - Vehicle-specific tools based on owner's recommendations
  - Essential toolset for roadside repairs
  - Socket set, wrenches, pliers, multimeter
  - Emergency supplies: zip ties, duct tape, wire, hose clamps
  - Work gloves and lighting
  
- **Auto Parts Store Visit:**
  - Fluids (oil, coolant, brake fluid, power steering)
  - Common replacement parts (belts, hoses, filters)
  - Specific parts needed for identified issues
  - Basic electrical supplies (fuses, wire, connectors)

### Evening: First Mechanical Session
- Find a quiet corner of your hotel parking lot
- Perform initial assessment and essential maintenance:
  - Check/change all fluids
  - Inspect belts and hoses
  - Test electrical systems and lights
  - Address any immediate concerns

### Overnight: Downtown Salt Lake City
- Check into budget-friendly hotel
- **Evening Activity:** Take a sunset drive to Ensign Peak for city panorama and first photos with the car
- **Documentation:** Create "Day 1" video discussing the purchase and initial impressions

---

## Day 2: Vehicle Preparation & Salt Flats

### Early Morning: Bonneville Salt Flats Adventure (2 hours west of SLC)
- Depart early to reach the salt flats by sunrise
- Create dramatic photos/videos of your project car on the iconic salt flats
- Test the car's performance in this unique environment
- **Photo Opportunity:** Capture the car against the vast white landscape

### Mid-Day: Return to Salt Lake City
- Stop at the **Larry H. Miller Automotive Museum** to see rare sports cars and racers
- **Lunch Break:** Find a local diner to discuss plans and modifications

### Afternoon: Final Preparations
- **Last Supply Run:**
  - Address any remaining parts needs identified during morning drive
  - Consider storage solutions for tools and supplies in the car
- **DIY Session:** Complete any remaining maintenance tasks
- **Route Planning:** Mark auto parts stores along your entire route on GPS/maps
- **Workspace Research:** Identify potential DIY-friendly locations for repairs along your journey

### Evening: Salt Lake City
- Pack the car with all supplies and prepare for departure
- **Car Culture:** Meet up with local car enthusiasts (arrange through forums/Facebook)
- **Documentation:** Film detailed "what we bought" reveal and day 2 progress

---

## Day 3: Salt Lake City to Moab

### Morning: Departure
- **Morning Ritual:** Film "cold start" sequence for YouTube content
- Begin journey east (~4 hours to Moab)

### En Route Highlights
- **Western Mining & Railroad Museum** in Helper, UT
- Take the scenic UT-128 along the Colorado River for the final approach to Moab
- **Parts Backup:** Make note of auto parts stores in Price and Green River
- **Photo Stop:** "Hole N' The Rock" home - a 5,000 sq ft home carved into solid sandstone

### Evening in Moab
- Check into accommodations (motel or campground)
- **Evening Drive:** Tour the main strip and scout photography locations
- **Maintenance Check:** Perform quick vehicle inspection after first day's journey
- **Documentation:** Record day's driving impressions and vehicle performance

---

## Day 4: Arches National Park

### Pre-Dawn: Sunrise Photography
- Enter Arches National Park before sunrise
- Capture morning light on the red rocks with your car
- **Photo Locations:** Park Avenue and Windows Section for iconic car/landscape photos

### Day Exploration
- Drive the scenic park roads
- Short hikes to key formations including Delicate Arch and Landscape Arch
- Picnic lunch amid the stunning scenery

### Evening in Moab
- **Technical Session:** Address any issues that arose during park driving
- **Photography:** Set up night sky photography with the car silhouetted against the stars
- **Documentation:** Time-lapse of the sunset against the red rocks with your car

---

## Day 5: Moab to Durango via Monument Valley

### Morning Departure
- **Drone Footage:** Capture your departure from the red rock country (if you have a drone)
- Take the scenic route through Monument Valley (adds 2-3 hours but worth it)

### En Route Experiences
- **Monument Valley:** Stop for iconic western landscape photos with the car
- **Four Corners Monument:** Stand in four states simultaneously
- **Gateway Auto Museum** in Gateway, CO: See one of the west's best car collections

### Evening in Durango
- Check into accommodations
- **Car Culture:** Check local calendars for "Cruisin' Durango" events
- **Evening Activity:** Stroll historic downtown Durango
- **Maintenance:** Address any issues that developed during the longer drive

---

## Day 6: Million Dollar Highway & Colorado Scenery

### Full-Day Adventure
- Drive the spectacular Million Dollar Highway (US-550) through Silverton to Ouray
- **Engineering Appreciation:** Learn about the incredible feat of building these mountain roads
- **Car Performance:** Document how your vehicle handles mountain passes and elevation changes
- **Photo Opportunities:** Multiple scenic pullouts for dramatic mountain/car photography

### Optional Experience
- Durango & Silverton Narrow Gauge Railroad short excursion
- **Alternative:** Visit Ouray County Museum to learn about the region's history

### Evening Return to Durango
- **Performance Analysis:** Compare how the car handled high altitude driving
- **Technical Session:** Make any needed adjustments based on mountain driving experience
- **Documentation:** Record segment discussing the engineering challenges of mountain driving

---

## Day 7: Durango to Albuquerque

### Morning Extension
- Optional visit to Mesa Verde National Park to see ancient cliff dwellings

### Scenic Route to Albuquerque
- Take NM-44 through Chaco Canyon instead of the direct route
- **Timing Plan:** Arrange arrival into Albuquerque for sunset when the Sandia Mountains turn pink

### Evening in Albuquerque
- Check into accommodations
- **Evening Activity:** Explore historic Old Town
- **Sandia Peak:** Consider taking the tramway for panoramic views
- **Maintenance Check:** Prepare vehicle for tomorrow's long drive

---

## Day 8: Albuquerque to Oklahoma City via Route 66

### Morning Activities
- **Breaking Bad Locations:** Visit filming sites if you're fans of the show
- **Route 66 Museum** in Santa Rosa, NM - worth a stop on your way east

### Route 66 Heritage Drive
- Drive preserved Route 66 sections through Tucumcari, NM and beyond
- **Amarillo Stop:** Photograph your car at Cadillac Ranch
- **Texas Tradition:** Lunch at the Big Texan Steak Ranch (home of the 72oz steak challenge)
- **Roadside Americana:** Blue Whale of Catoosa near Tulsa
- **Auto History:** Heart of Route 66 Auto Museum in Sapulpa, OK

### Evening in Oklahoma City
- Check into accommodations
- **Maintenance Session:** Thorough check after the long drive
- **Documentation:** Record segment about the Route 66 experience and car's performance

---

## Day 9: Oklahoma City to Nashville

### Morning Departure
- **Local History:** Oklahoma Route 66 Museum in Clinton before hitting the road
- **YouTube Content:** Film morning coffee and "Will it start?" sequence

### Breaking Up the Drive
- **Memphis Detour:** Short stop at Beale Street for music history and BBQ lunch
- **Car Testing:** Document how the vehicle handles after a week on the road
- **Route Variation:** Take slightly northern route through the scenic Ozark Mountains

### Evening in Nashville
- Check into centrally located hotel
- **Evening Experience:** Plan arrival to catch the music scene with specific honky-tonks for first-time visitors
- **Maintenance Check:** Address any issues before the final leg home

---

## Day 10: Nashville to Huntsville

### Morning in Nashville
- **Auto Enthusiast Stop:** Visit Lane Motor Museum to see unique and strange vehicles
- **Car Cleaning:** Visit self-service car wash to clean up project car for "arrival" photos
- **Final Checks:** Ensure everything is ready for the last leg home

### Scenic Route Home
- Take the Natchez Trace Parkway for part of your journey instead of I-65
- **Historic Stop:** Jack Daniel's Distillery in Lynchburg for a final tourist activity

### Arrival in Huntsville
- **Coordination:** Have family/friends meet at a predetermined location for a welcome celebration
- **Final Documentation:** Take "journey complete" photos in the same poses as your departure photos
- **Celebration:** Reflect on the completed journey and successful revival
