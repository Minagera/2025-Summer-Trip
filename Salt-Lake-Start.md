# Revive & Drive Road Trip Itinerary – Starting in Salt Lake City, UT

This itinerary is designed as a memorable, bonding road trip with your son. You’ll fly one-way from Huntsville using Delta SkyMiles, land in Salt Lake City, and then pick up or finalize your project car before driving back home. The planned route mixes scenic byways, state/national park visits, and cultural stops along well-maintained highways.

**Trip Duration:** ~10 Days  
**Total Driving Distance (approx.):** ~1,600 miles  
**Vehicle Note:** Focus on a classic project car from the late ’70s to early ’90s. If 4WD is available, use it only on designated scenic/off-road portions.

---

## Day 1: Arrival in Salt Lake City

- **Flight:** Arrive in Salt Lake City, UT (HSV → SLC using SkyMiles)
- **Arrangements:** Pick up any pre-arranged rental/loaner vehicle or meet with your project car seller.
- **Evening:**  
  - Check into a budget-friendly hotel in downtown Salt Lake City.
  - Explore local attractions such as **Temple Square** or a stroll near the **Utah State Capitol**.
- **Dinner:** Try a local eatery for a hearty meal and discuss the trip ahead.

---

## Day 2: Finalizing the Project Car & Local Exploration

- **Morning:**  
  - Meet with sellers or visit a local restoration shop to inspect/finalize your vehicle.
- **Afternoon:**  
  - Visit **Antelope Island State Park** at the Great Salt Lake for breathtaking scenery.
- **Evening:**  
  - Return to Salt Lake City, enjoy dinner, and rest up for the drive ahead.

---

## Day 3: Salt Lake City to Moab

- **Departure:** Leave early for Moab (~4-hour drive).
- **Along the Way:**  
  - Enjoy wide-open desert vistas on I-15/US-6.
- **Arrival in Moab:**  
  - Check into a local motel or set up camp.
  - Explore Moab town and relax.
- **Optional:**  
  - Short walk or drive to local points of interest.

---

## Day 4: Exploring Arches National Park

- **Morning to Afternoon:**  
  - Spend the day in **Arches National Park**. Must-sees include the Delicate Arch and Landscape Arch.
  - Pack a picnic lunch to enjoy amidst the stunning sandstone formations.
- **Optional Off-road:**  
  - If your car’s capability permits, consider a short guided off-road scenic drive.
- **Evening:**  
  - Return to Moab, relax and review the day’s photos and memories.

---

## Day 5: Moab to Durango, Colorado

- **Morning:**  
  - Depart Moab for Durango (~3–4 hours drive).
- **In Durango:**  
  - Stroll through historic downtown with its charming shops and local museums.
  - Optionally visit a small car museum or meet local classic car enthusiasts.
- **Overnight:**  
  - Stay in a modest hotel or motel.

---

## Day 6: Scenic Drive in Colorado

- **Plan:**  
  - Enjoy a scenic drive along one of Colorado’s designated byways or heritage routes.
- **Options:**  
  - Consider a short excursion on a heritage railway (e.g., Durango & Silverton Narrow Gauge Railroad) for a different perspective.
- **Evening:**  
  - Return to Durango for dinner and rest.

---

## Day 7: Durango to Albuquerque, New Mexico

- **Morning Departure:**  
  - Drive from Durango to Albuquerque (~4–5 hours).
- **In Albuquerque:**  
  - Explore the historic **Old Town**, local galleries, and cultural shops.
  - Consider riding the **Sandia Peak Tramway** for panoramic views.
- **Overnight:**  
  - Book a stay in a budget-friendly hotel or motel.

---

## Day 8: Albuquerque to Oklahoma City (via Amarillo Stop)

- **Morning:**  
  - Begin the drive toward Oklahoma City (~7 hours total).  
- **Detour:**  
  - Stop in **Amarillo, TX** to snap photos at quirky attractions like **Cadillac Ranch**.
- **Evening:**  
  - Arrive in or near Oklahoma City and check into accommodations.
  - Enjoy a filling dinner and relax.

---

## Day 9: Oklahoma City to Nashville, TN

- **Morning Departure:**  
  - Drive from Oklahoma City to Nashville (~8 hours).  
- **In Nashville:**  
  - Explore the cultural scene, enjoy some local music, or simply grab dinner at a casual restaurant.
- **Overnight:**  
  - Stay overnight in Nashville.

---

## Day 10: Nashville to Huntsville, AL

- **Final Leg:**  
  - The drive is short (~1.5–2 hours).  
- **Wrap-up:**  
  - Arrive in Huntsville and celebrate the journey. Take time to reflect on the bonding experience and the progress on the project car.

---

## Additional Tips

- **Flexibility:**  
  - Build in optional stops if your project car needs quick repairs. Have a list of repair shops along major highways.
- **Safety & Comfort:**  
  - Alternate between budget hotels, motels, and campsites as needed.
- **Documentation:**  
  - Keep a travel journal or create a video log to capture moments with your son.
