# Revive & Drive Road Trip Plan

This is my plan for a father-son road trip combining automotive adventure with scenic exploration. We'll fly one-way to Salt Lake City, pick up the pre-purchased project car, and drive it all the way back to Alabama while fixing it up along the way.

**Trip Duration:** Summer 2025 (10 Days)  
**Total Driving Distance:** ~1,900 miles  
**The Vehicle:** Classic project car (late '70s to early '90s) that I've already arranged to purchase

## Pre-Trip Planning

### Before We Go
- **Flights:** Booked our one-way flight to SLC
- **Forum Research:** Join model-specific forums to ask about common issues with this vehicle
- **Community Connections:** Post our route on enthusiast forums to find potential local help if needed
- **Tools:** Check what specialized tools might be needed for our specific vehicle
- **Documentation:** Set up action camera mounts and prepare storage for video/photo files
- **Packing:** Bring basic clothing but save most luggage space for tools and parts we'll acquire
- **Weather:** Check forecasts for all regions we'll drive through
- **Shop Research:** Bookmark restoration shops along our route for emergency support

### Salt Lake City Classic Car Resources
I found several shops in Salt Lake we can call if we run into trouble:
- **Red Line Restoration** - They specialize in GM and Mopar muscle cars and trucks
- **Kindig-it Design** - The shop from "Bitchin' Rides" TV show (by appointment only)
- **Dan Green Restorations** - Experts in preserving classic and vintage automobiles
- **Wasatch Customs** - Specializes in vintage car repairs and custom builds

## Day 2: Vehicle Acquisition & Preparation

### The Big Morning
- **Meeting the Seller:**
  - Go through a thorough inspection together
  - Document all known issues with notes and photos
  - Take it for a test drive to identify quirks and concerns
  - Get specific recommendations about immediate repairs
  - Complete the paperwork and payment
  - Make a prioritized list of needed parts/tools

### Parts Run
- **Tool Shopping:**
  - Get vehicle-specific tools based on seller's recommendations
  - Basic toolset for roadside repairs
  - Socket set, wrenches, pliers, multimeter
  - Emergency supplies: zip ties, duct tape, wire, hose clamps
  - Work gloves and lighting
  
- **Auto Parts Store:**
  - Fluids (oil, coolant, brake fluid, power steering)
  - Common replacement parts (belts, hoses, filters)
  - Parts for specific issues we identified
  - Basic electrical supplies (fuses, wire, connectors)
  - Pick up a repair manual if available for this model

### First Wrenching Session
- Find a quiet area to work
- Perform initial assessment and maintenance:
  - Check/change all fluids
  - Inspect belts and hoses
  - Test electrical systems and lights
  - Address any immediate concerns

### First Night with the Car
- **Evening Drive:** Take a sunset drive for city panorama and first photos with the car
- **Dinner:** Try a local brewpub or restaurant
- **Documentation:** Create our "Day 1" video discussing the purchase and initial impressions

## Day 7: Million Dollar Highway & Colorado Scenery

### Mountain Adventure
- Drive the spectacular Million Dollar Highway (US-550) through Silverton to Ouray
- **Engineering Focus:** Learn about the incredible feat of building these mountain roads
- **Performance Test:** See how our vehicle handles mountain passes and elevation changes
- **Photo Ops:** Stop at multiple scenic pullouts for dramatic mountain/car photography

### Must-See Stops
- **Coal Bank Pass:** Elevation 10,640 ft - First major pass with excellent viewpoints
- **Molas Pass:** Elevation 10,970 ft - Stop at Molas Lake for stunning mountain reflections
- **Silverton:** Historic mining town with preserved Victorian architecture
- **Red Mountain Pass:** Elevation 11,075 ft - The highest point with views of oxidized red mountain slopes
- **Uncompahgre Gorge:** Stunning canyon with the highway clinging to steep cliff walls
- **Bear Creek Falls:** Beautiful waterfall viewpoint just before reaching Ouray
- **Ouray:** Known as the "Switzerland of America" with 19th century architecture

### Must-Do Activity
- **Durango & Silverton Narrow Gauge Railroad:** Definite plan to ride this historic train (2-hour round trip)
- **Local Food:** Try a Colorado green chili burger at Handlebars Food & Saloon in Silverton
- **Coffee Stop:** Coffee Bear in Silverton for excellent coffee before tackling the mountain passes
- **Optional:** Visit Ouray County Museum to learn about the region's history

### Return to Durango
- **Performance Review:** Analyze how our car handled the high altitude driving
- **Technical Session:** Make any needed adjustments based on mountain driving experience
- **Dinner:** Find a steakhouse for classic Western steaks
- **Documentation:** Record our segment discussing the engineering challenges of mountain driving
- **Driving Note:** We'll drive Silverton to Ouray direction for the inside lane position (less scary)

## Day 9: Albuquerque to Oklahoma City via Route 66

### Morning Activities
- **Breaking Bad Sites:** Visit filming locations if we're fans of the show
- **Classic Car Museum:** Visit Route 66 Auto Museum in Santa Rosa, NM on our way east

### Route 66 Heritage Drive
- Drive preserved Route 66 sections through Tucumcari, NM and beyond
- **Tucumcari:** Stop for photos at the iconic Blue Swallow Motel neon sign (Route 66 gem since 1939)
- **Hidden Gem:** Glenrio Ghost Town at the Texas/New Mexico border
- **Amarillo Stop:** Photograph our car at Cadillac Ranch (bring spray paint to leave our mark)
- **Texas Must-See:** Consider detour to Palo Duro Canyon State Park - the "Grand Canyon of Texas"
- **Texas Tradition:** Lunch at the Big Texan Steak Ranch (home of the 72oz steak challenge)
- **Oklahoma Classic:** Stop at the U-Drop Inn in Shamrock, TX (inspiration for Ramone's in "Cars")
- **Route 66 Museum:** Visit Oklahoma Route 66 Museum in Clinton for history of the Mother Road
- **Roadside Americana:** Blue Whale of Catoosa near Tulsa
- **Must-Stop:** POPS 66 in Arcadia - iconic gas station, restaurant and soda ranch with 700+ soda flavors and a 66-foot soda bottle sculpture (perfect for fueling up, snacks, and photos)
- **Auto History:** Heart of Route 66 Auto Museum in Sapulpa, OK (special event: Model T driving experiences!)

### Oklahoma City Evening
- Check into our accommodations
- **Local Experience:** Visit Bricktown Entertainment District for dinner
- **Route 66 Heritage:** Drive historic alignments through OKC with landmarks like the Gold Dome Building
- **Car Culture:** If timing aligns with "Coffee and Cars OKC" (typically first Saturday mornings), check their schedule
- **Maintenance Session:** Do a thorough check after the long drive
- **Documentation:** Record our segment about the Route 66 experience and car's performance
