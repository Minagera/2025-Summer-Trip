# Revive & Drive Road Trip Itinerary – Salt Lake City to Huntsville

This father-son road trip combines automotive adventure with scenic exploration. You'll fly one-way to Salt Lake City, pick up a pre-purchased project car, and embark on the journey of reviving it while driving back home.

**Trip Duration:** June 13-22, 2025 (10 Days)  
**Total Driving Distance:** ~1,900 miles  
**Vehicle:** Pre-purchased classic project car (late '70s to early '90s)

## Pre-Trip Planning

### Before You Go
- **Flight Details:** HSV to SLC on Friday, June 13th, 2025
- **Forums & Research:** Join model-specific forums to ask about common issues with your vehicle
- **Community Connections:** Post your route on enthusiast forums to find potential local help
- **Tool Assessment:** Check what specialized tools might be needed for your specific vehicle
- **Documentation Prep:** Set up GoPro mounts, prepare storage for video/photo files
- **Packing List:** Bring basic clothing, but save luggage space for tools and parts you'll acquire
- **Weather Planning:** Check forecasts for all regions you'll travel through in mid-June
- **Local Resources:** Research classic car restoration shops along your route for emergency support

### Salt Lake City Classic Car Resources
- **Red Line Restoration** - Specializes in GM and Mopar muscle cars and trucks
- **Kindig-it Design** - Featured on "Bitchin' Rides" TV show (by appointment only)
- **Dan Green Restorations** - Experts in preserving classic and vintage automobiles
- **Wasatch Customs** - Specializes in vintage car repairs and custom builds

## Day 2: Saturday, June 14 - Vehicle Acquisition & Preparation

### Morning: The Handover
- **Meet with Seller (3+ hours):**
  - Thoroughly inspect the vehicle together
  - Document all known issues (take notes and photos)
  - Test drive to identify quirks and concerns
  - Get specific recommendations for immediate repairs
  - Complete paperwork and payment
  - Create a prioritized list of needed parts/tools

### Afternoon: Supplies Run
- **Harbor Freight Shopping:**
  - Vehicle-specific tools based on owner's recommendations
  - Essential toolset for roadside repairs
  - Socket set, wrenches, pliers, multimeter
  - Emergency supplies: zip ties, duct tape, wire, hose clamps
  - Work gloves and lighting
  
- **Auto Parts Store Visit:**
  - Fluids (oil, coolant, brake fluid, power steering)
  - Common replacement parts (belts, hoses, filters)
  - Specific parts needed for identified issues
  - Basic electrical supplies (fuses, wire, connectors)
  - Consider picking up a Haynes/Chilton manual for your specific car

### Evening: First Mechanical Session
- Find a quiet corner of your hotel parking lot
- Perform initial assessment and essential maintenance:
  - Check/change all fluids
  - Inspect belts and hoses
  - Test electrical systems and lights
  - Address any immediate concerns

### Overnight: Salt Lake City
- **Evening Activity:** Take a sunset drive to Ensign Peak for city panorama and first photos with the car
- **Local Dinner:** Try Squatters Pub Brewery or Red Iguana for authentic local food
- **Documentation:** Create "Day 1" video discussing the purchase and initial impressions

- **Last Supply Run:**

  - Consider storage solutions for tools and supplies in the car
- **DIY Session:** Complete any remaining maintenance tasks
- **Route Planning:** Mark auto parts stores along your entire route on GPS/maps
- **Workspace Research:** Identify potential DIY-friendly locations for repairs along your journey

### Evening: Salt Lake City
- Pack the car with all supplies and prepare for departure
- **Car Culture:** Try to connect with local car enthusiasts through forum meetups
- **Documentation:** Film detailed "what we bought" reveal and day 2 progress

## Day 7: Thursday, June 19 - Million Dollar Highway & Colorado Scenery

### Full-Day Adventure
- Drive the spectacular Million Dollar Highway (US-550) through Silverton to Ouray
- **Engineering Appreciation:** Learn about the incredible feat of building these mountain roads
- **Car Performance:** Document how your vehicle handles mountain passes and elevation changes
- **Photo Opportunities:** Multiple scenic pullouts for dramatic mountain/car photography

### Million Dollar Highway Must-See Stops
- **Coal Bank Pass:** Elevation 10,640 ft - First major pass with excellent viewpoints
- **Molas Pass:** Elevation 10,970 ft - Stop at Molas Lake for stunning mountain reflections
- **Silverton:** Historic mining town with preserved Victorian architecture
- **Red Mountain Pass:** Elevation 11,075 ft - The highest point with views of oxidized red mountain slopes
- **Uncompahgre Gorge:** Stunning canyon with the highway clinging to steep cliff walls
- **Bear Creek Falls:** Beautiful waterfall viewpoint just before reaching Ouray
- **Ouray:** Known as the "Switzerland of America" with 19th century architecture

### Optional Experience
- Durango & Silverton Narrow Gauge Railroad short excursion (2-hour round trip)
- **Alternative:** Visit Ouray County Museum to learn about the region's history
- **Local Specialty:** Try a Colorado green chili burger at Handlebars Food & Saloon in Silverton
- **Recommended Stop:** Coffee Bear in Silverton for excellent coffee before tackling the mountain passes

### Evening Return to Durango
- **Performance Analysis:** Compare how the car handled high altitude driving
- **Technical Session:** Make any needed adjustments based on mountain driving experience
- **Local Dinner:** The Ore House for classic Western steaks
- **Documentation:** Record segment discussing the engineering challenges of mountain driving
- **Driving Tip:** Consider driving Silverton to Ouray direction for the inside lane position (less scary for nervous drivers)

- **Route 66 Landmark:** Drive Central Avenue to see the iconic Route 66 neon sign and vintage motels

## Day 9: Saturday, June 21 - Albuquerque to Oklahoma City via Route 66

### Morning Activities
- **Breaking Bad Locations:** Visit filming sites if you're fans of the show
- **Classic Car Museum:** Visit Route 66 Auto Museum in Santa Rosa, NM on your way east

### Route 66 Heritage Drive
- Drive preserved Route 66 sections through Tucumcari, NM and beyond
- **Tucumcari:** Stop for photos at the iconic Blue Swallow Motel neon sign (a Route 66 gem since 1939)
- **Hidden Gem:** Glenrio Ghost Town at the Texas/New Mexico border
- **Amarillo Stop:** Photograph your car at Cadillac Ranch (bring spray paint to leave your mark)
- **Texas Must-See:** Consider detour to Palo Duro Canyon State Park - the "Grand Canyon of Texas"
- **Texas Tradition:** Lunch at the Big Texan Steak Ranch (home of the 72oz steak challenge)
- **Oklahoma Classic:** Stop at the U-Drop Inn in Shamrock, TX (inspiration for Ramone's in "Cars")
- **Route 66 Museum:** Visit Oklahoma Route 66 Museum in Clinton for history of the Mother Road
- **Roadside Americana:** Blue Whale of Catoosa near Tulsa
- **Auto History:** Heart of Route 66 Auto Museum in Sapulpa, OK (June 2025 special event: they're offering Model T driving experiences on Route 66!)

### Evening in Oklahoma City
- Check into accommodations
- **Local Experience:** Visit Bricktown Entertainment District for dinner
- **Route 66 Heritage:** Drive historic alignments through OKC with landmarks like the Gold Dome Building
- **Car Culture:** If timing aligns with "Coffee and Cars OKC" (typically first Saturday mornings), check their schedule
- **Maintenance Session:** Thorough check after the long drive
- **Documentation:** Record segment about the Route 66 experience and car's performance
